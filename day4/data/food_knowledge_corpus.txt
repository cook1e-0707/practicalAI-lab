Question: What is the food safety danger zone?
Verified answer: The danger zone is generally 40 to 140 degrees Fahrenheit (4 to 60 degrees Celsius), where bacteria can grow quickly.
Source: FDA: Cooking—Food Safety for Moms to Be
End record.

Food Knowledge Record 1224
Topic: Nutrients
Question: What does vitamin C help the body do?
Verified answer: Vitamin C acts as an antioxidant, helps make collagen for wound healing, improves absorption of iron from plant foods, and supports normal immune function.
Source: NIH ODS: Vitamin C Fact Sheet for Consumers
End record.

Food Knowledge Record 1225
Topic: Food safety
Question: Why should raw meat be kept separate from ready-to-eat food?
Verified answer: Raw meat and its juices can spread illness-causing bacteria to food that will not be cooked.
Source: USDA FSIS: Ground Beef and Food Safety
End record.
